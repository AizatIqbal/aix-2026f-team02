## Issue #3 — AI Assistant Service Requirement

**Goal (Spec) / Spec**

* **Input:** Business owner asks a question about their business data or business decisions.
* **Processing:** The AI assistant provides a response based on the question.
* **Output:** A response to the business owner's question.
* **Failure Condition:** The requested data is unavailable or insufficient to answer the question.

### Requested Prompt Summary / Prompt (summary)

Asked AI to help create an acceptance criterion for an AI assistant service that allows a business owner to ask questions about their data or business decisions.

### Decisions on the outcome

**Rejected, because:**

* The team did not use the AI-generated result in the final specification.
* The team decided to create the requirement based on our own discussion and project needs instead.

### Verification Method / How it was verified

The team compared the AI-generated result with the project's requirements and discussed whether it should be used. The AI result was not included in the final acceptance criterion.

**Adoption Status:** Rejected
